Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

1. Introduction

AI Content Creator ("the Service") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our content management tool.

2. Information We Collect

The Service may collect and process the following information:

  • API Credentials: API keys and access tokens you provide to connect to third-party services (OpenAI, TikTok, ElevenLabs). These are stored locally on your machine and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Generated Content: Scripts, images, audio, and videos generated through the Service are stored locally on your device.
  • Usage Logs: The Service maintains local logs of video generation history (timestamps, niches, upload status) for your monitoring purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Generate and publish video content on your behalf
  • Authenticate with third-party APIs using your provided credentials
  • Maintain local activity logs for your monitoring and review

4. Data Storage and Security

All data, including API keys, generated content, and logs, is stored locally on your machine or in your private GitHub repository. The Service does not operate external servers and does not transmit your personal data to any party other than the third-party APIs required for content generation and publishing.

5. Third-Party Services

The Service interacts with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • OpenAI: For script generation, image creation, and text-to-speech
  • TikTok: For video publishing via the Content Posting API
  • ElevenLabs (optional): For enhanced voiceover generation

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these services.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Data is only sent to the third-party APIs listed above as required to provide the Service's functionality.

7. Data Retention

Generated content and logs are stored locally until you choose to delete them. Temporary files created during video generation are automatically deleted after processing.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data stored by the Service (all data is local)
  • Delete any generated content or logs at any time
  • Revoke API access tokens at any time through the respective third-party platforms
  • Stop using the Service at any time

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
